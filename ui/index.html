3 · How to use it

  • Ctrl + Alt hold: talk, let go to send. A quick tap opens this notch to type.
  • Ctrl + Alt + L: work with me (live mode) follows a call and helps when it can.
  • Ctrl + Enter: paste the answer into the app you were in. Esc stops or hides.
  • Do things: “open Chrome and go to GitHub”, “attach the report from Documents and send it to my team in Teams”. Every step shows here; sending, deleting, buying or running something waits for your OK.
  • Schedules: “every weekday at 9 open my calendar and summarise today”, “remind me at 5 to send the report”.
  • The gear (hover the notch) opens Settings. Drag the notch by its top strip to any screen edge.
